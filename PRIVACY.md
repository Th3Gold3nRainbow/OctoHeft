# Privacy Policy for OctoHeft

**Last Updated:** November 2026

OctoHeft is an open-source parallel download accelerator built with Rust and Tauri. We believe in complete transparency and user privacy.

### 1. Data Collection
OctoHeft does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identifiable information (PII), telemetry, analytics, or browsing habits.

### 2. Network Activity & File Transfers
All network operations performed by OctoHeft occur strictly between your local device and the remote web servers you choose to download from via HTTP, HTTPS, or FTP:
* Download URLs, transfer speeds, and file paths remain strictly on your local machine.
* OctoHeft does not route your network traffic through any intermediary proprietary servers.
* No logging or tracking of downloaded files is performed externally.

### 3. Local Storage
OctoHeft stores local configuration settings (such as default download directory, maximum parallel connections, and queue state) exclusively on your local device. This data is never synchronized with remote servers.

### 4. Third-Party Services
OctoHeft does not bundle third-party tracking libraries, advertising SDKs, or external diagnostic frameworks.

### 5. Contact
If you have any questions or feedback regarding this privacy policy, please open an issue on the official project repository:
https://github.com/Th3Gold3nRainbow/OctoHeft/issues
